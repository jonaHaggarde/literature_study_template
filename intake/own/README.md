# intake/own/

What you wrote yourself, as input to the project: your own brief, notes, a list of
constraints, a description of your situation. Anything handed to you by someone else goes in
`../received/`.

`source-access.md` is here because it describes *your* access to sources, and anyone who
forks the template has to rewrite it.

Text and markdown are read directly by `/intake`. A PDF of your own is converted next to
itself, the same as in `received/`. Do not put decisions, ideas or explainers here: they have
their own folders (`../../decisions.md`, `../../ideation/`, `../../theory/`).
