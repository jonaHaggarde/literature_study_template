Gates

What happens at a gate

1

Open

Starts the stop; nothing is passed yet. The article stands still while you read.

2

Briefing

In chat: facts from a script, then Claude's own critique, both short.

3

Check

/understand asks questions you answer from memory. Claude ends with ready or slower.

4

Close

Passes the gate. You say so, and the article is accepted with its version recorded.

Claude opens or closes a gate only when you tell it to. The check never blocks a gate, and it is left out for the definition and the requirements.

The check exists to make you slow down and think about what was produced. Whatever you remembered correctly needs no follow-up. What you did not understand is either explained or you reread the article. Its result is only a suggestion, ready or slower, with no effect on the gate.
