The idea

What the template does

You say what the study is about. Claude finds papers or reads the ones you bring, builds a searchable, citation-grounded catalog, and checks its own coverage against your scope.

Catalog

Every paper summarised once, tagged, with its methods recorded as cards.

Scope

A definition you accept fixes what counts as in scope before any paper is gathered.

Gaps

Thin areas show up against the scope, so the study cannot pretend to be complete.

Literature study template · 2

The template is built for one study at a time, not a shared knowledge base. It is topic-agnostic in content, but opinionated about the method: scope first, derived files never edited by hand, every claim traceable to a source.
