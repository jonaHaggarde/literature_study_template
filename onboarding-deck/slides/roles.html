Who does what

You

Answer the intake interview, read each article, and decide when the study moves on.

Claude

Interviews you, drafts the articles, writes the notes, briefs you at a gate, and says what it is unsure of.

Scripts

Convert PDFs, count, check and draw the status picture.

Claude never decides a gate, and changes an accepted article only when you ask.

The split is deliberate. Anything a script can do deterministically is a script, so it costs nothing and cannot drift. Claude is for judgment: reading, drafting, critique. You hold the decisions.
