Welcome

This is a local dashboard over your literature study: an exploration map of the problem space, a browser for the gaps still worth researching, a milestone checklist, and a console to talk to Claude directly. This page is the orientation stop — what each piece is for and how a study grows through them.

Getting started

Every study starts one of two ways: describe what you want to study directly (once the Console page is connected to Claude), or drop material — a brief, a proposal, anything handed down from outside — into project_documents/ so scope can be set from it first. Either way, scope comes first: it's what later tells the map and the gaps browser what counts as in-scope versus a distraction.

From there, exploration begins: identify the big stages of the problem, then branch each one out — broad (comparing alternative approaches to the same problem) or deep (digging further into one specific method). Branches you commit to get marked chosen; everything else stays visible rather than disappearing, so the map always shows both the path taken and what it was chosen from.

The four views

Exploration Map is the source of truth: a tree of stages and branches, each carrying a status (explored, identified, evaluated-and-not-favored, or chosen) and, for any branch with its own sub-branches, a color-intensity readout of how much of it is still unexplored. Gaps is a filtered, listable view over that same tree — just the still-open leaves — built for triage and picking a batch to research together, not for editing structure. Milestones is a separate, independent checklist tree for tracking progress toward "done," with its own evaluate-and-cache mechanism. Console is a direct line to Claude, terminal-style, for anything that isn't a click-through action on one of the other three.

Where this stands right now

This whole dashboard is still a frontend-only prototype: every page persists its own state in this browser's localStorage, and nothing here has called Claude or a backend yet. See ../../gui-dev-notes.md at the repo root for the running log of what's built, what's decided, and what's still open.
